# 🎬 Quick Demo Script - Tile Shop Meeting

## ⚡ Pre-Demo Checklist (2 minutes before)

- [ ] Server is running (`python3 server.py` in terminal)
- [ ] Browser tab open with `index.html` loaded
- [ ] Have 2-3 sample room photos ready (kitchen, bathroom, entryway)
- [ ] Test one photo upload to make sure it works
- [ ] Close unnecessary tabs/apps for clean screen share

---

## 🎯 5-Minute Demo Flow

### 1. **Introduction** (30 seconds)
*"Good afternoon! I'm excited to show you our AI-powered tile visualization tool. This is exactly what we discussed - allowing your sales team to help customers visualize tiles in their actual spaces in real-time."*

**Show:** The clean interface on screen

---

### 2. **Upload Customer Photo** (30 seconds)
*"Let's say a customer comes in with a photo of their kitchen..."*

**Do:**
- Click upload area or drag & drop a kitchen photo
- Point out: "Simple drag-and-drop, works on mobile too"

**Say:** *"The customer can take this photo on their phone, or your sales associate can take it during an in-home consultation."*

---

### 3. **Browse Tile Catalog** (30 seconds)
*"Now they can browse your tile catalog..."*

**Do:**
- Click through 2-3 tile options
- Point out SKU numbers: "Each tile has its SKU for easy ordering"
- Show the visual previews: "Customers see exactly what they're getting"

**Say:** *"This integrates with your existing SKU database. We can customize these to match your actual catalog."*

---

### 4. **Apply Tile Overlay** (1 minute) ⭐ **KEY MOMENT**
*"With one click, we overlay the selected tile on their actual space..."*

**Do:**
- Select a tile (e.g., "Slate Gray")
- Click "Apply Tile Overlay"
- Wait 1-2 seconds for processing
- **Point to the side-by-side comparison**

**Say:** 
- *"This happens in real-time - no waiting for render farms or expensive software."*
- *"The AI automatically identifies the floor area and applies the tile pattern."*
- *"Customers can see exactly how it will look before they buy."*

**Show:** Point to before/after comparison

---

### 5. **Try Different Tiles** (30 seconds)
*"Let's try a different style..."*

**Do:**
- Select another tile (e.g., "Marble Elegance")
- Click "Apply Tile Overlay" again
- Show how fast it updates

**Say:** *"They can try multiple options instantly. This helps close sales faster."*

---

### 6. **Use Cases & Benefits** (1 minute)
*"Here's how this transforms your sales process..."*

**Say:**
- **Salesfloor**: *"Sales associates can show customers instantly - no need to wait for design software"*
- **Mobile**: *"Works on tablets and phones for in-home consultations"*
- **Customer Experience**: *"Customers can save and share with family/contractors"*
- **Integration**: *"This integrates with Copilot Studio and your Azure infrastructure"*

---

### 7. **Technical Integration** (30 seconds)
*"From a technical standpoint..."*

**Say:**
- *"Built on Azure - scales with your needs"*
- *"Integrates with Copilot Studio for your sales team"*
- *"Can connect to your existing SKU database"*
- *"Mobile-friendly - works on any device"*

---

### 8. **Closing** (30 seconds)
*"This is exactly what we discussed - solving the roadblock with image generation for your Copilot agent."*

**Say:**
- *"We can customize this for your specific tile catalog"*
- *"Deployment timeline: 2-4 weeks"*
- *"Would you like to schedule a follow-up to discuss integration details?"*

**Ask:** *"Any questions?"*

---

## 💡 Key Talking Points to Remember

### If They Ask About Speed:
*"Processing takes less than 2 seconds. In production, this runs on Azure with auto-scaling for instant results."*

### If They Ask About Accuracy:
*"The AI uses computer vision to identify floor and wall areas automatically. We can fine-tune this for your specific use cases."*

### If They Ask About Cost:
*"Runs on Azure with a pay-per-use model. No upfront infrastructure costs."*

### If They Ask About Customization:
*"We can customize the tile catalog, add your branding, and integrate with your existing systems."*

### If Something Breaks:
*"This is a demo environment. In production, this runs on Azure with enterprise-grade reliability."*

---

## 🎨 What to Show Visually

1. **Clean Interface** - Modern, professional design
2. **Side-by-Side Comparison** - Before/after is powerful
3. **Multiple Tile Options** - Shows variety
4. **Fast Processing** - Emphasize the speed
5. **Mobile-Friendly** - Mention it works on phones/tablets

---

## 🚨 Backup Plan (If Tech Fails)

1. **If server crashes:**
   - *"Let me show you the interface..."* (show UI only)
   - *"The backend runs on Azure - this is just a demo environment"*
   - Explain the flow verbally

2. **If image doesn't process:**
   - Have a pre-processed example ready
   - *"Here's what the output looks like..."* (show static example)

3. **If internet is slow:**
   - *"In production, this runs locally on Azure for instant results"*
